Privacy

Privacy policy.

How MARKETEAM collects, uses and protects your personal information, in accordance with Québec’s Law 25.

Last updated: 2026-09-28

01. Who is responsible

MARKETEAM is a Québec business. The person with the highest authority within MARKETEAM acts as the person in charge of the protection of personal information.

For any question, write to us using the form on the Contact page.

02. What we collect

Only what you give us in the contact form: your name, your company (optional), your email or phone, your industry, what you’re interested in, your message and the site language.

We collect no other information without your knowledge.

03. Why we use it

To respond to your request, prepare a proposal and follow up on our conversation. We do not sell, rent or share your information for advertising purposes.

04. Your consent

We ask for your consent before the form is sent, and we keep the date of that consent. You can withdraw it at any time by writing to us.

05. Who has access

Only authorized members of the MARKETEAM team, through password-protected access. Our hosting providers do not use your information for their own purposes.

06. How long we keep it

As long as needed to handle your request and, where applicable, manage our business relationship. Requests that don’t move forward are deleted after 24 months.

07. Cookies

This site uses a single functional cookie that remembers the language you choose. No advertising cookies or audience measurement tools are used. If that changes, we will ask for your consent first.

08. AI assistant

The site offers an artificial intelligence assistant that answers questions about MARKETEAM. Your messages are sent to OpenAI (United States) only to generate the reply, with the request that they not be stored to train its models.

MARKETEAM does not keep your conversations: we only keep an anonymous daily message count to prevent abuse. Don’t include personal information. Answers are generated automatically and may contain errors.

09. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, and withdraw your consent. We reply within 30 days.

You can also file a complaint with the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec.

10. Security

Communication with the site is encrypted (HTTPS) and access to the requests we receive is limited and protected. No system is perfect: in the event of a confidentiality incident, we will act in accordance with the law.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date of the last update appears at the top of this page.
